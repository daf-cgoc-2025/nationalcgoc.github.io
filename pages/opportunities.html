Mentorship Program Manager

  • DAF CGOC
Role and responsibilities

Leads the DAF CGOC Mentorship Program, connecting cadets and newly commissioned Lieutenants with experienced company grade officers (CGOs). The program helps participants build professional networks, develop as officers, and navigate the transition from commissioning through their first months at their initial duty station.

  • Oversee and maintain the central matching platform.
  • Screen mentor and mentee application submissions.
  • Execute and monitor pairings.
  • Safeguard Personally Identifiable Information (PII) and uphold program security controls.
  • Track program health metrics, participant volume, and match status progression.
  • Collect and evaluate participant feedback.
  • Provide program training, outreach materials, and implementation guidance to United States Air Force Academy (USAFA), Air Force Reserve Officer Training Corps (AFROTC), and Officer Training School (OTS) cadre to build awareness of the program and generate a strong, diverse mentee pool.

Secretary, Eastern Region

  • Region support staff, Eastern Region
Role and responsibilities

The Eastern Region Secretary shall document the minutes of all base CGOC leadership and Executive Board meetings, notify all members of the meetings as required (i.e. calendar invites and reminders), and have custody of all CGOC records. The Secretary shall submit the Region CGOC’s official meeting minutes to the DAF CGOC. The Secretary shall collect the base CGOCs’ Quarterly Activity Reports (QARs) following the most recent standard established by the DAF CGOC and submit them (or provide to the Region Director for submission) to the DAF CGOC by the established suspense.

Initiatives — Get Involved

All Initiatives Live

The DAF CGOC is dedicated to developing future and current junior officers across the globe and supporting DAF senior leaders. Below are total force initiatives and ways to support today.

Active Initiative

First Term Officer Course

FTOC equips first-term officers with the practical skills, mentorship, and reference materials needed in their first assignment. The Implementation Team welcomes new contributors year-round — from curriculum support to facilitation.

FTOC Resources Join the Implementation Team
Active Initiative

Thinktank

The DAF CGOC Thinktank puts CGOs in research cohorts with Air University. Cohort research is published and presented to senior leaders. Senior leaders with ideas to submit are encouraged to reach out.

Visit the Thinktank I Want to Learn More
Active Initiative

Penpal Program

The DAF CGOC Mentorship Program pairs cadets and new Lieutenants with an experienced CGO at their first duty station. Guidance, not oversight. AFROTC and USAFA cadre can refer cadets directly, and sign-up takes about two minutes.
